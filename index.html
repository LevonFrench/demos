    Deterministic, beat-driven WebGPU visual performance system. Sixteen authored scenes, layered generators and effects, live audio analysis, tempo-aware direction, and shareable presets. Drop a track or use the built-in test signal.

    Audio-reactive WebGL. Three scenes — reactive type, a geometric tunnel, a synthwave landscape — with 19 stackable effects, fluid and particle solvers, and a sequencer locked to the detected tempo. Plays whatever you feed it: loopback, mic, or a dropped file.
