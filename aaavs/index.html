aaavs

A layered, beat-driven visualiser. Feed it audio, stack layers, and let the director cut presets on the bar.

Get audio in

drop a filedrag any audio file onto the page. No permissions needed.
load .avsopen L, then Load a Winamp AVS preset to run its legacy ordered graph.
Tbuilt-in 128 BPM test signal — kick, hat and a sustained pad, so both transient and sustained sources have something to read

Transport

Spacepause / resume without losing the audio-clock position
← →skip one musical bar backward / forward
Lthe layer panel also exposes all three transport controls

Presets

Pauto preset changes, responsive to energy and onsets on 2–12 bar boundaries
, .previous / next preset
Llayer panel — preset bank, per-layer blend, opacity, clock, envelope

Effects

1–9kaleidoscope segments (1 bypasses)
Ktoggle the kaleidoscope
[ ]trail length, in beats

Diagnostics

Hhud — fps, tempo lock, scheduler lateness, GPU time
Ddebug overlay — waveform, spectrum, spectrogram, stereo field, onset classes

Worth knowing

level 0.00normal between transients — most of a percussive track is silence.
