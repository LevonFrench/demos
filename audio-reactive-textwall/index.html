pulse

An audio-reactive visualiser. Feed it sound, then stack effects on top.

1 · Get audio in

Drop a file
Easiest. Drag any audio file onto the page — no permissions needed.
Find inputs
Lists recording devices. A loopback device (Stereo Mix, CABLE Output, your interface’s Loopback) is your speaker mix exposed as an input. Best option if you have one — the permission sticks. Detected ones are marked ●.
Screen / tab audio
What you pick in the share dialog decides whether there is any audio:
  • A Chrome tab — works everywhere. Good for YouTube, SoundCloud.
  • Entire screen — works on Windows. The only way to capture a desktop app like the Spotify client.
  • A single window — never carries audio. This is usually why “system audio doesn’t work”.
DRM tabs such as Spotify Web capture silent — use Entire screen for those.
Mic
Point it at a speaker. Works, but picks up the room.

2 · Play with it

Fire effects
Press 1–8, 9 0, or QWERTYUIO. They stack, and every one reverses itself back to rest.
Auto-fire
The effect buttons choose what auto-fire may pick. Lit = eligible. Clicking one never fires it — the key still does, armed or not. Shift+key arms one, X flips them all.
Mouse
Drag on the canvas to shove the wall. Hold to swirl.
